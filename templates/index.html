FEATURE 1 — ACCI

Ambient Conversational Context Injection

Instead of guessing between 660 possible gestures, EchoSign listens to your conversation partner and reduces the problem to just 3 likely candidates — before any gesture is even detected.

Search space: 660 → 15 → 3 candidates
Mathematical gain: 80% → 94% top-1 accuracy
FEATURE 2 — ECHO LOOP

Confirmation Echo Self-Training

After TTS output, EchoSign captures 2 seconds of ambient audio and uses Sentence-BERT to check if your partner's response semantically matches. No feedback button. No effort.

cosine_similarity > 0.75 → CONFIRMED → confidence += 0.1
Accuracy: 42% → 94% over 10 conversations
